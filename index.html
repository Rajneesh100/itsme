01 / 06 Robotics · Multi-agent · GNN

HAWK

A swarm of interceptor drones and a sky full of moving target drones. An interceptor has to close in until the target is inside its interception range, and the swarm works out among itself who goes after which target.

The spark

Deciding which drone chases which target is usually done with greedy rules or auctions. I wanted to see if a small graph neural network, where drones pass messages to their neighbours, could learn it from noisy local sensor data instead.

Plot twist

It didn't win. Greedy and Auction intercepted all 6 targets in 4.28 s and the GNN took 8.24 s. In a 2 minute continuous run it still intercepted all 35 targets with zero collisions, and I kept the honest numbers in the README.

03 / 06 Reinforcement learning 🚧 still evolving

Rocket Rally

Race cars that taught themselves to race on a bumpy, jumpy rally track, and then learned to shoot each other with rockets.

The spark

It started as a sim for a WALL-E-style wheeled robot I want to build for real one day. Then I gave the robot a racetrack… and it turned into a racing game.

What the cars taught me

Scale what works before getting clever. My fancy LSTM and curriculum run lost badly. Plain PPO with a 360° lidar and twice the training steps was the first car to finish a rally lap without crashing, in about 22 s. After that came league mode, where cars also get rewarded for overtakes and Burnout-style takedowns. Then I gave them rockets.

05 / 06 Distributed systems · Edge

Durable Chat

Slack, but every channel is its own tiny server with its own SQLite database, living at the edge. No database cluster at all.

The spark

Chat apps usually mean a big shared database and a pub/sub layer. Cloudflare Durable Objects promised "one object per room, with storage built in." I wanted to see how far that goes.

How it's built

A pure functional core, (state, event) → [nextState, effects], wrapped in a thin shell that does the I/O. WebSocket hibernation means an idle room costs nothing, and auth is PBKDF2 with plain Web Crypto instead of an auth service.

06 / 06 Imitation learning

Next-Frame Driver

A car that only sees a 6×6 patch of the world learns to drive through an endless obstacle field by copying a pathfinding teacher.

The spark

Can a tiny network learn "where to go next" from nothing but examples? I let an A* planner drive first (it builds its map as it explores and replans when it bumps into surprises), recorded every move, then trained a small CNN to imitate it.

Trick that helped

Every recorded frame flipped and rotated is another valid lesson, so I got 4× the training data without any extra driving. A JEPA style world model is in progress on the side.
